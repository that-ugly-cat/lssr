{#- The earmark: a dot on a record, one per reviewer, with an optional why. Two macros, because the mark has two halves that belong in two different columns. `dot()` is yours — it goes at the head of the title, where the eye lands when scanning a long list, because the mark is a property of the paper and not of a decision. `others()` is everybody else's, and it goes in the column that already collects what everyone thinks of this record. Everybody's marks are visible to everybody: the margin of a shared copy, not a diary. What that costs at screening 1 is written in models.py on the Earmark class and said plainly in the guide — the votes stay blind, an argued note does not, and a reviewer who writes a decision in here has gone around their own blinding. -#} {#- Your dot, and your note when it is raised. `my_mark` is your own row or None; keeping the lookup outside the macro means the caller does it once per row instead of the macro filtering a list twice. -#} {% macro mine(ws, r, my_mark) %}
