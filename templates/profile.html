MCP keys

A key lets a model client (Claude Code, Claude Desktop) read your reviews from inside a conversation: records, votes, criteria, extraction, synthesis. It reaches exactly the reviews you reach, and stops there.

Reading is all a key does unless you say otherwise. With writing enabled it can also cast title-and-abstract screening votes, in your name, marked [via MCP] in the reason. Nothing else writes: extraction, adjudication, screening 2 and marking a step done stay here. Writing belongs to the key rather than to you, so the keys you already have stay readers, and it is decided when the key is minted and never after — to change your mind, revoke it and mint another.
