✅ include · 🤔 maybe · ❌ exclude · ⚠️ conflict · ❓ pending — cast your own vote on the right. maybe parks a record: it doesn't go to full text until someone moves it to include/exclude. Screening is blind: you see the other reviewers' votes only after you've voted, or once an adjudicator has closed the record. Conflicts are resolved by the owner.

Alt+click a vote button to say why. A plain click votes and leaves any reason you wrote before untouched; Alt+click opens a box first, and the vote is cast when you save it — Enter saves, Shift+Enter breaks the line, Esc closes without voting. Worth doing on the records where the decision is not obvious from the abstract: the reason travels into the conflict, into the export and into whatever the next reader makes of it.

≠ divergent lists every record where at least one voice differs from another, the model's included — conflict only ever means two humans disagreeing, so a record the model reads differently, or parks as maybe, never appears there.
