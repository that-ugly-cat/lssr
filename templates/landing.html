LSSR

Living Systematic Scoping Review

An entire scoping review in one tool — and a living one: every step re-runs on demand and updates the results without redoing the human work that was already there.

What it does
The query, translated where it has to go

Query building and source-aware translation to fourteen databases, with direct harvest for the four that have an open API, and deduplication across them.

Screening, blind and in more than one pair of hands

Several reviewers who cannot see each other's calls, and a conflict resolution step that stays human.

Full text, by degrees

A retrieval ladder that tries the routes in order, and structured extraction with an AI first draft — to be corrected, not accepted.

What comes out

Citations assembled procedurally from the retrieved records, a PRISMA diagram that keeps itself current, and per review a public read-only dashboard you can share without giving anyone an account.

Readable by an assistant too

An MCP surface with its own revocable key, so a question about the corpus can be asked where it comes up. It reaches only the reviews its owner reaches, and it reads: every decision stays a human one, made here.
